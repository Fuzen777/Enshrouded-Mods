# Streamlined Logistics Features

## Gem Forge

Show Linked Gems in Gem Forge is on by default and works independently of Craft From Linked Chests. Loose gems from linked chests appear in the forge’s Gems list for insertion, upgrading, or recycling. Your backpack pays the cost and receives recycled essence.

## Skill Resets

With Craft From Linked Chests enabled, a Flame Altar skill reset or an individual skill refund can spend runes from linked chests. Kiln uses the backpack and the current base’s Magic Chests first, then takes the shortfall from linked chests. This requires Kiln 0.2.0-dev.31.18 or newer.
