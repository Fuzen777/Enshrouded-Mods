# Grimoire - Spells and Arrows Slots

Kiln mod (C#, id `grimoire`) that keeps spells and arrows out of the backpack in slots of their own, equipped and listed in the HUD in the player's own order (the equipped stack takes one backpack slot). Source in `src`, tests in `tests\GrimoireRulesTests` (the rules without the game). `Scripts\Build.ps1` runs the tests and builds the mod into `dist\grimoire`; with `-Install` it copies it to `<game>\kiln\mods\grimoire` (game closed). The settings are in Kiln's profile (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Kiln\profiles\my-mods\config\grimoire.json`), so an install never touches them. Tags are `grimoire/vX.Y.Z`.

It was the spellbook of Better Spells (3.3.0-dev.1 to dev.11, 23 and 24 Nov 2026); that history, with what was found in game, is in `..\Better Spells\CHANGES.md`.

## 1.2.1 (not uploaded)

- **Fix: the HUD spell list no longer flickers when switching to a grimoire spell, and scrolls smoothly, also on dedicated servers** (the user's report, and a Nexus report that on a dedicated server the list could not be scrolled without stopping on each spell, 27 Nov 2026). The cause: every switch is a swap (the picked spell into the backpack cell, the old one into the grimoire), the game builds its list again on each backpack change, and Kiln lays the grimoire's rows only over the build they were made for. For the build after a swap the HUD showed the game's own rows (kiln.log, 26 Nov 2026: `game rows [Eternal Shroud Meteor x0, Eternal Ball Lightning I x1]`) with the cursor back on the equipped spell, until the grimoire published again a frame later. On a dedicated server the swap comes back a round trip after the pick, when the player has scrolled on, so the cursor jumped back to the spell the server had just equipped and the next scroll step started from there.
- **HUD Switch Delay** (new setting, 250 ms by default, 0 to 1000): a pick in the HUD list is sent only once the list's cursor has stayed on the entry that long. Kiln moves the cursor by itself and equips nothing, so scrolling past entries changes nothing in the backpack and the list does not rebuild; only the entry the player stops on is swapped in, once. 0 equips each entry as the cursor reaches it (as before). In the F3 window under Hide Other Spells And Arrows, and on the mod's page. A click in the F3 window still equips at once, and cancels a HUD pick still waiting.
- **No more x0 on the spell just switched away from**: the rows built for the game's new list came from the book's view before the swap, which still had the old spell equipped and took the game's count 0 of its row (revision 19 in the same log: `Eternal Shroud Meteor x0`). `GrimoireRules.HudRows` now keeps the view's count of the equipped stack when another entry has stock in the game's rows (the game has moved on), and shows an entry the game holds with stock at least at that stock (tested both ways: the game's rows newer than the view, and the view newer).
- The one rebuild left at the switch itself still shows the game's rows for a frame: that needs Kiln (keep a Replace provider's last rows over a new list revision until it publishes again), in the Kiln Report Tracker. Not tested in game yet.

## 1.0.3 (not uploaded)

- **The grimoire and Better Spells' settings window keep together** (the user's report and asks, 26 Nov 2026, with Better Spells 1.1.3; Kiln has no window grouping, so both mods do it with `UiWindow.MoveTo`, mod API 0.2.20). `BesideBetterSpells` (Grimoire) and `WindowToGrimoire` (Better Spells) are replaced by one class, `WindowPair`, the same file in both mods with `WindowPairRules` (its geometry, tested) and `WindowPlacement` (now also the same file in both).
- Both windows now tell each other where they are (`grimoire.window` and `better-spells.window`: left, top, right, bottom as fractions of the viewport, then the seconds since the window opened and since the player last moved it), when that changes and every half second, and null when they close. Better Spells tells the grimoire where it is going as it opens; a grimoire that opened without knowing yet goes beside it on the first message within a second.
- **Docked windows move together**: while the two touch side by side, moving or resizing one moves the other with it (it follows a frame or two behind: the messages go through Kiln's tick). Hold **Shift** while dragging to move one alone; a window dropped within 16 pixels of the other's side snaps against it (tops lined up when they are that close too) and docks again. Of two docked windows that no longer touch, the one the player moved last stays and the other follows, so the two never chase each other (neither moved yet: Better Spells stays). Shift is read with `KeyInput.Listen` (a press or repeat within the last second counts as held, so a release lost to alt-tab does not leave it stuck).
- A place a window got beside or following the other one is not the player's: opened alone, it goes back to where the player left it (as 1.0.1 did for the grimoire, now for both). A window the player moves with their own drag keeps that place.
- On a Kiln before mod API 0.2.20 nothing moves, as before. Not tested in game yet.

## 1.0.2 (not uploaded)

- **Fix: Grimoire works on dedicated servers and for players who join another's world** (reported on Nexus, 26 Nov 2026: on a dedicated server F3 opened the grimoire, but "In Your Backpack" stayed empty and nothing could be added). 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 ran every step for the local player of a game that hosts its world: on a dedicated server the server had no local player and the player's game had no world (Kiln's inventory calls only work where the world runs), so nothing ran on either side. The server's kiln.log showed the storage restored and the slot changes of the player, and nothing else.
- Grimoire now has two halves. The **server half** runs where the world runs (a dedicated server, or the game hosting it) and does the steps for every player whose game has Grimoire: picks, add, take out, reorder, running out and the filing back, each player with their own queue entry, waiting pick and settling. After each change it sends the player's game a view of the book (entries, counts, the backpack's spells or arrows, the equipped one) and its toasts. The **player's half** draws the screen and the HUD lists from that view and sends its steps to the server, over Kiln's network channel `grimoire:net` (version 1, not required: a player without Grimoire joins as before). In a world the game hosts, both halves are in one game and call each other directly, as before.
- Each player's settings are their own: a joined game sends Mod Enabled and the slot counts to the server when it joins and when they change (the defaults until it did). Hide Other Spells And Arrows stays on the player's game.
- The server leaves players without Grimoire on their game alone (no filing back of spells they can't see).
- A joined game has no item catalog, so Ctrl+click there asks both books and the server's book of the other kind says nothing. Item names are the player's game's own (its language).
- Joined to a server without Kiln or without Grimoire, the screen says so instead of listing nothing.
- Needs Grimoire on the server (or host) and on each player's game that wants a grimoire. Not run on a real dedicated server yet.

## 0.2.1 (not released)

- **Fix: switching as fast as the game allows no longer leaves spells behind** (seen by the user in 0.2.0, 25 Nov 2026: copies left on the action bar that were never taken back, several spells in the backpack, a spell gone from the grimoire's list until added again). kiln.log showed three causes:
  - **Steps ran late and out of order.** A pick that came while a swap was running was retried every 250 ms, so a burst of picks ran in no set order, some of them seconds late. Steps now run one at a time in the order they came, and a pick replaces a pick still waiting: only the latest choice is equipped (the game can also raise the same pick several times within a few milliseconds).
  - **The game sometimes empties the Spell slot right after a swap** (11:33:05: Shock Wisp equipped at `11417:5`, the slot empty 80 ms later, with no pick of the mod's in between), with that spell still in the backpack. The next pick then took the picked spell out of the storage into a second backpack cell, and every such switch added one more. Now a grimoire spell still loose in the backpack takes the slot back first, where it lies, and the switch is a swap in that cell; one second after the last change of the slot, an empty slot gets its last spell back.
  - **Spells on the action bar could not be filed back**: Kiln refuses a plain move from the action bar ("the slot is on player 1's action bar"), and the equipped cell was an action bar cell (`11417:5`). Filing back now moves from the action bar too, and happens at the end of every switch and one second after the last change of the slot (was five seconds), so the backpack keeps one grimoire spell, the equipped one.
- A grimoire spell loose in the backpack keeps its entry and its place in the grimoire's list and the HUD list until it is filed back (it used to drop to the end of the HUD list, or off the grimoire's list).
- Ctrl+click and Add also take a spell from the action bar.

## 0.1.3 (not released)

- **Fix: no spell can be lost any more; the equipped spell takes one backpack slot again** (the user's choice of three, 24 Nov 2026, with a Kiln report so it can go back to none). In 0.1.2 the user found switching broken (the HUD snapped back to the first backpack spell) and Eternal Pillar Of Flames gone. kiln.log: the Pillar was equipped in the Equipment page cell `10385:7` when the game was quit at 06:35, and at the next load it was nowhere (not equipped, not stored, not in the backpack). - **Bringing back what 0.1.0 to 0.1.2 left in the Equipment page**: at every pick, take out, add and settle (and 5 s after the storage is restored at load), an equipped spell in an Equipment page cell is unequipped into the backpack by the game's own unequip and equipped again there, and one that isn't equipped is filed into the storage.
- A grimoire spell the game switched away from (a backpack spell picked from the game's own rows) stays in the backpack and is filed back 5 s later; the player's own backpack spells stay. A spell is the grimoire's while it is in the player's order or stored; Take Out removes it from the order.
- The four-times-a-second count check of 0.1.1 is gone: with the equipped spell in the backpack, the game's own rows carry its live count again.

## 0.1.0 (not released)

- **Moved out of Better Spells** (asked 24 Nov 2026: Better Spells stays on spell settings, and the book will hold arrows too). Built on what Better Spells 3.3.0-dev.11 tested: Kiln's player storage (`Items.RegisterPlayerStorage`, Kiln 0.2.0-dev.31), here `grimoire:spells`, 32 slots, spells only, kept on death. The book kept in character data (dev.8) is dropped. Nothing moves over from Better Spells: its storage `better-spells:spellbook` was emptied by the user on 24 Nov 2026 at 05:41 (kiln.log).
- **F3 opens the grimoire** (Better Spells moved to Shift+F3, the user's choice). The screen lists the spells in the player's order: click to equip, drag to reorder, right-click (or drag onto the backpack list) to take out; the backpack's spells below it, click or drag them in to add; Ctrl+click a spell in the game's inventory adds it; Add All and Take All Out. **Mod Enabled** and **Spell Slots** (1 to 32, default 12) are at the bottom of the screen and on the mod's page in Kiln's menu.
- **The HUD's spell list follows the grimoire's order** (`Ui.AmmoList`, Replace), then the game's other rows. The order is kept with the character (`CharacterData.Carried`, key `order-spells`), because a swap from the storage puts the old spell into the picked slot and the storage's own order changes on every switch (found in dev.10, fixed in dev.11).
- **Fix: the equipped spell no longer takes a backpack slot.** Seen by the user in dev.11. The log showed why: the game equips a spell where it lies, and a swap from the storage puts the new spell into the cell of the spell equipped before (every pick went to the same backpack cell, `10582:2` or `10582:6`). Kiln's GiveEquip only creates a stack in the Equipment page while the slot is empty (`give_equip_run` in Kiln's native items service reuses an equipped cell, Generic cells included for arrows, spells and bait). So when the equipped spell lies in a backpack cell, the mod unequips it, gives the same stack into the Equipment page and takes the backpack stack (Reseat); an empty slot gets the picked spell given there and its stored stack taken; later swaps then land in the Equipment page's cell.
- **Running out**: when a charged spell runs out, more of it from the grimoire is equipped, else the next spell in the order.
- **A spell the game switched away from** (a backpack spell picked from the game's own rows) is left in the Equipment page where nothing shows it; five seconds after the last change of the slot it is filed back into the grimoire.
- Works in a world you host (single player or the host in co-op), as before.

> [!warning]
> Not seen in game yet. Not known until tried: whether the game's unequip keeps the spell in the backpack and empties the slot (Reseat needs both), whether a swap from the storage then lands in the Equipment page's cell, and whether a stack left in an Equipment page moves into the storage (`MoveOptions.Equipped`). kiln.log has every step under "spells:".
